New here? How choosing works

  1. 1
    Check the tasks

    The AI drafted them from the grading criteria. Change any title or weight, move a task to another criterion under "Details", or add and delete tasks.

  2. 2
    Pick your tasks

    The numbers 1 to 5 are the weight, the effort: 1 ≈ an hour, 3 ≈ a day, 5 ≈ several days. Your fair share is all points ÷ the team size, so "2 / 6" means you chose 2 of your fair 6 points.

  3. 3
    Send to GitHub

    Once every task has a person, send them: each task becomes an issue assigned to that person. From then on, closing issues in GitHub moves the board.
